# Privacy Policy for YouTube Ad Auto-skipper

YouTube Ad Auto-skipper is an open-source browser extension. It automates interactions with YouTube advertisements, including clicking available "Skip Ad" buttons, closing overlay advertisements, and optionally muting advertisements.
This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension processes, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## 1. Information We Process

The extension processes limited information necessary to provide its functionality.

### YouTube Page Content

When visiting YouTube, the extension accesses relevant webpage elements to:

- Detect advertisements and available "Skip Ad" buttons.
- Automatically skip advertisements when possible.
- Close overlay advertisements.
- Control advertisement muting according to user preferences.
- Identify YouTube channels for channel-specific settings.

This information is processed locally within the browser to provide the extension's functionality.

### User Preferences

The extension stores configuration settings, which may include:

- Advertisement skipping preferences and delays.
- Advertisement muting preferences.
- Channel-specific settings and associated channel information.

These preferences are stored locally using the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage

The extension does not intentionally collect or transmit users' personal information to external servers.
User preferences are stored locally within the browser. They are not synchronized to a service account or server.
The extension does not include developer-operated analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising services.
The extension does not request access to passwords, payment information, personal communications, or unrelated browsing activity.

## 3. Third-Party Services and External Resources

The extension operates on YouTube, a service provided by Google. YouTube itself may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy.
Certain interface elements may load external resources, such as Google Fonts or YouTube channel images. When these resources are requested, their providers may receive technical connection information, such as IP addresses and browser-related request information.
Such requests are handled by the respective third-party providers and are not used by the extension developer for analytics, profiling, or advertising.
For more information, please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the extension is used exclusively to provide its advertised functionality, including automated advertisement interactions and user-configurable preferences.

The extension does not:

- Sell user information.
- Use user information for personalized advertising.
- Share user preferences with data brokers.
- Build user profiles for tracking or marketing purposes.
- Use information for creditworthiness assessments or lending decisions.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored settings remain in the browser until they are changed, reset, or deleted.
Users can manage their preferences through the extension's settings interface.
Users can also remove the extension and clear its associated local browser storage through their browser's extension management and data controls.
The developer does not maintain a remote database containing users' extension preferences.

## 6. Data Security

The extension uses standard browser extension APIs for local data storage and processing.

It is designed to minimize access to information and limit its operations to the functionality described in this policy.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the extension's functionality, data handling practices, or applicable requirements.
However, the extension will always adhere to the principle of data minimization, ensuring that only the minimum amount of data strictly necessary for its functionality is processed.
Updates will be published in this repository.
